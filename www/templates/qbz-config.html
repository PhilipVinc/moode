Output
$_qobuz_output_chain Qobuz playback goes through the same output chain as MPD, so whatever is set in Audio Config — Output mode, CamillaDSP, the equalizers, crossfeed, multiroom — applies to it too. There is nothing to set here.

For bit-perfect playback set Audio Config > ALSA options > Output mode to Direct and leave the DSP stages off. Tracks then reach the DAC at their own rate. With anything inserted in the chain the stream is converted to the rate that chain runs at, which is the point of inserting it.

How the Qobuz app's volume slider is applied:
• Software — scales the samples. Works with any output, but the audio is no longer bit-perfect.
• Locked at 100% — keeps the stream at full scale and ignores the app's slider, so volume must come from moOde, your amplifier or your DAC. This is the setting to use for bit-perfect playback.
Adjusts the volume of all songs played so that they sound as though they are of equal loudness.
Playback
Whether a track that is not already cached starts as soon as enough of it has arrived, or waits for the whole file:
• As soon as buffered — playback starts in a second or two instead of tens of seconds.
• After the full track downloads — steadier on an unreliable connection, at the cost of a long pause before every new track.
Whether finished tracks are kept on disk for instant replay:
• Stream only, do not cache — writes nothing to the SD card. A hi-res track is 60–80 MB, so this spares the card over a long listening session. Gapless playback is not possible without the cache.
• Cache tracks on disk — replaying a track is instant, and gapless playback works because the next track can be loaded ahead of time.
How much RAM the renderer may hold decoded tracks in. Auto works this out from how much memory this player has and is almost always the right answer.

Lower it only if this player is doing something else that needs the memory. A hi-res track is around 120 MB, and a track larger than the cache is not cached at all — so below that, gapless playback of hi-res tracks stops working, because gapless needs the next track held ready.
Play consecutive tracks without a gap of silence between them. Needs Track cache set to keep tracks on disk: the next track has to be loaded ahead of time, which is not possible when nothing is cached. $_gapless_hint
What to do when a track cannot be played at the selected quality, either because your subscription does not include it or because your DAC cannot do that rate:
• Play at a lower quality — the track still plays.
• Skip the track — quality stays guaranteed, at the cost of silent gaps in an album. This matters more with Direct routing, where there is no conversion step to fall back on.
Account
Advertise this player on the network so any Qobuz app can cast to it without a login. The app hands the player its own session when a device is selected; the most recent cast takes over, like Spotify Connect. Turn this off to log the player into a fixed Qobuz account instead.
